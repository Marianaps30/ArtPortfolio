01 / About Mariana

❧ · ❧

Mariana
Paro e Silva.

MP
artist + maker

I am Mariana Paro e Silva, a 17-year-old senior at WFHS and a trilingual multidisciplinary artist interested in turning ideas into places, characters, objects, and experiences.

I love teaching art, making video games, and building worlds from the inside out. My work moves between concept art, character design, maps, fashion and costume design, accessories, UI/UX, VR/AR ideas, and 3D modeling.

I am especially interested in the Interactive Design and Game Development major, information design, computer science, and the way visual systems can make an experience feel clear, playful, and alive.

Community + work
